01 — Brand Essence

WHO WE ARE

01
The Concept

Bermuda is named for the feeling of disappearing — where the outside world ceases to exist and only the music remains. Not the triangle as a place of danger, but as a place of beautiful, willing surrender. You go in, and you don't want to come back. That's the experience we create on every dancefloor.

02
The Sound

Tech house with soul. Driving, rhythmic, relentless — but warm underneath. We sit at the intersection of groove and euphoria. Tracks that make your hips move before your brain registers why. Sets that build slowly and pay off hard. The kind of music that feels like a memory you haven't made yet.

03
The Feeling

Euphoric and a little intoxicating. Warm like golden hour. Confident without being cold. We're the DJs who are genuinely having the time of their lives behind the decks — and you feel that. The energy is infectious. The crowd is the performance. We play with them, not at them.

04
The Personality

Three real people. Witty, self-aware, and a little audacious. We don't take ourselves too seriously — but we take our craft very seriously. Think John Summit's rawness, filtered through a warm tropical lens. We post like friends, perform like professionals, and build like a brand.

Color Usage Rules

90% of the time: Deep black backgrounds with Gold or Amber text/icons. This is your signature look.
Teal accent is used sparingly — booking CTAs, highlights, special announcements only. Never as a primary color.
Warm White is for body copy and secondary elements on dark backgrounds. Never use pure #FFFFFF — it's too harsh.
No light-mode posts. Every piece of content lives on a dark, rich background. Always.

03 — Typography

TYPE SYSTEM

Display / Headers
BERMUDA
Bebas Neue — Free on Google Fonts

All caps. Wide tracking. Used for the wordmark, section headers, event names, and any large display text. Never sentence case. Always commanding.

Editorial / Accent
Lost in the sound.
Cormorant Garamond Italic — Free on Google Fonts

Italic only. Used for taglines, captions, pull quotes, and mood statements. The contrast between Bebas and Cormorant is your visual signature — loud and quiet together.

Body / UI Copy

DM Sans Light — Clean, modern, unobtrusive. Used for bios, captions longer than one line, booking info, and any functional copy. Always light weight (300). Never bold in body copy.

05 — Voice & Tone

HOW WE TALK

WITTY
Self-aware humor. Never try-hard. Quick and clever, not cringe.
WARM
Community-forward. We celebrate the crowd, the culture, the scene.
CONFIDENT
No apologies, no over-explaining. We know who we are.
REAL
Three actual humans. We share the behind the scenes, the fails, the fire.
✓ Do say
"We don't make playlists. We make moments."
Bold, specific, memorable. Sounds like a brand.
✗ Don't say
"We are a DJ group that plays tech house music in Denver Colorado."
Functional but forgettable. No personality, no hook.
✓ Do say
"You went in for a quick drink. You left at 2am. You're welcome."
Witty, confident, speaks to the experience. Shareable.
✗ Don't say
"Had an amazing time tonight! Thanks to everyone who came out! 🙏🙏🙏"
Generic. Every DJ on the planet posts this. Zero differentiation.
✓ Do say
"The set was supposed to be 90 minutes. The crowd had other plans."
Tells a story. Implies demand. Makes people wish they were there.
✗ Don't say
"Please follow and like for more content! Hit the notification bell!"
Desperate energy. Build pull, not push. Let the content do the work.

06 — Content Strategy

CONTENT PILLARS

🎛️
The Music
35%
Mix clips, transition reels, track reactions, SoundCloud drops. The product. Make it visual and punchy — 15 to 30 seconds max.
🎬
Behind The Drop
30%
Setup process, soundcheck chaos, pre-show rituals, candid moments. This is where the personality lives. Raw and real beats polished every time.
🌐
Culture & Taste
20%
Tracks you're obsessed with, events you'd go to, artists you respect. Position yourselves as tastemakers, not just performers.
🏆
Social Proof
15%
Gig announcements, recaps, crowd energy, any press or features. Every gig — no matter the size — gets documented. A birthday party counts.
